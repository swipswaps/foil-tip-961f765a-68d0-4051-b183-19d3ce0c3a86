Etymology & Foil Tip Design

Mouche (French for fly) denotes a pinpoint training mark. Conventional foil points pair a spring-loaded plunger (>500 g break force) and scoring circuit inside a <500 g foil.

Mouche avoids fragile hot-air reflow harvesting of WLCSP earbud dies in favor of a thin wireless module, rigid-flex carrier, strain-relieved thin pouch cell, and isolated tip sense.

Accelerometer + Microphone Logging

Contact: Magnitude, jerk, squared window energy, and refractory gating paired with MEMS microphone transients and tip-switch state classify flicks, flat hits, and blade beats.

Spatial: Gyro/accel pose and short impact-anchored arcs log relative tip kinematics while guarding against double-integration drift.
