Etymology & Engineer's Tip Constraints

Mouche (French for fly) names a pinpoint training target. Foil tips compress inside tight millimeter barrel tolerances (>500 g tip break force) under a <500 g weapon mass limit—favoring a thin wireless module and rigid-flex carrier over fragile WLCSP earbud harvesting.

Accelerometer + Mic Spatial & Contact Log

Contact: Accel magnitude, jerk, squared window energy, and refractory gating paired with MEMS microphone transients and tip-switch state classify flicks, flat hits, and beats. Spatial: Gyro/accel pose and short impact-anchored arcs log relative motion while respecting integration drift limits.
