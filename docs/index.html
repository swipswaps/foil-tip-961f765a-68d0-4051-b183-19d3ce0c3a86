Etymology & Tip Design

Mouche (French for fly) refers to a precision target on a training surface or plastron. Existing foil tips pair a spring-loaded plunger (>500 g break force) and scoring circuit inside a <500 g foil.

Mouche avoids fragile hot-air reflow harvesting of WLCSP earbud dies in favor of a compact wireless module, rigid-flex carrier, strain-relieved thin pouch cell, and isolated tip sense.

Accelerometer + Microphone Logging

Contact: Accelerometer magnitude, jerk, and window energy with refractory gating plus MEMS microphone transients and tip-switch state log flicks, flat hits, and blade beats.

Spatial: Gyro/accel pose and short impact-anchored arcs capture relative tip kinematics while guarding against double-integration drift.
