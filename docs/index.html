Etymology & Foil Tip Context

Mouche (French for fly) denotes a small precision target on a training surface or plastron. Existing foil tips combine a spring-loaded plunger, barrel, and scoring circuit under strict FIE limits (<500 g total foil mass, >500 g tip spring break force) and tight internal tip volume.

Rather than fragile hot-air reflow harvesting of WLCSP earbud dies, Mouche favors an ultra-thin wireless module, rigid-flex carrier, strain-relieved thin pouch cell, isolated tip contact sense, and mechanical shock hardening.

Accelerometer + Microphone: Contact & Spatial Logging

  • Contact Logging: Accelerometer magnitude, jerk, and window energy separate flicks from flat hits with refractory gating, while a MEMS microphone captures acoustic/vibration contact transients (plunger seating, blade beats, surface impacts) alongside tip switch state.
  • Spatial Logging: Accelerometer + gyroscope orientation/pose and short-interval impact/motion-anchored arcs log relative tip kinematics, while explicitly accounting for double-integration drift when global room coordinates are not externally anchored.
